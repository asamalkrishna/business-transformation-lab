About me

Curious by default. Independent by nature.

The professional portfolio tells you what I do. This page is about the person who does it.

A little about me

Always looking for something new to understand.

I would describe myself as curious, ambitious and analytical, but also fairly calm and independent. I like learning new things — preferably one or two at a time rather than trying to become an expert in everything simultaneously.

I enjoy building things, solving difficult problems, having the freedom to figure things out and doing work that has a visible impact. Outside work, that curiosity usually turns into travel, technology, F1, gaming, music or a completely random conversation that somehow lasts two hours.

CalmI generally prefer thinking things through rather than adding unnecessary drama to them.
What drives me

Build. Learn. Solve. Make an impact.

I am motivated by difficult problems, learning something genuinely useful, having freedom in how I work and seeing that something I built or changed actually matters.

  • I like building: this website is one example.
  • I like solving: especially problems where the answer is not obvious at the start.
  • I like learning: but I work better when curiosity has some focus.
  • I like freedom: enough space to think, experiment and take ownership.
What people tend to say

Approachable, friendly and loyal.

I am usually the person organising trips for my friends, and I enjoy long conversations about completely random topics. I can be introverted, but that does not mean I dislike people — I just prefer good conversations to constant noise.

And yes, I am adventurous enough to travel solo, then come home and immediately start planning the next trip.
