Bhagavad Gita

Words I genuinely return to

A couple of ideas from the Gita that I genuinely try to carry with me — especially when outcomes are outside my control.

02 · 47
कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन ।
English translation

“You have a right to your actions, but not to the results of those actions.”

For me, it is a reminder to focus on what I can control — the effort, the decisions and the work — rather than becoming consumed by the outcome.

02 · 48
सिद्ध्यसिद्ध्योः समो भूत्वा समत्वं योग उच्यते ॥
English translation

“Remain balanced in success and failure; such equanimity is called yoga.”

I like this one because things do not always go according to plan. Staying grounded when something works — and when it does not — is something I try to practise.
