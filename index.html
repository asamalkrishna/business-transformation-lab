About Me
// WHY THIS SITE EXISTS

Most portfolios show finished dashboards. This one shows the whole path to get there — starting from a business question, not a chart. Every case study follows the same structure: business context, stakeholders, the process as it actually works, data understanding, root cause analysis, KPI design, recommendations, and a business case.

I work full-time as a Business Analyst at RWE Supply & Trading in Essen, Germany, focused on counterparty onboarding, master data, and KPI reporting. The case studies here are drawn from that work and from my time at Emteria — real projects, not simulated ones.
